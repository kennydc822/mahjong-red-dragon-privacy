常見問題

點樣玩「紅中變」？

紅中（中）係百搭萬能牌，可以代替任何牌湊成順子或刻子；如果手上集齊四隻紅中，即時自摸獲勝。

點樣同朋友連線對戰？

主選單揀「多人遊戲」，建立房間後將房號分享俾朋友，或者輸入朋友嘅房號加入同一房間。

遊戲收費嗎？

本遊戲完全免費遊玩，內含廣告。純屬娛樂，不涉及任何真實金錢博彩。

點樣轉語言？

主選單 > 設定 > 語言，可選擇繁體中文、簡體中文或 English。

連線中斷點算？

請檢查網絡連線後重新加入房間。如問題持續，請電郵聯絡我們並附上大概發生時間。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the "Red Dragon Wild" rules work?

The red dragon is a wild joker that can substitute for any tile to complete a run or triplet. Collect all four red dragons in your hand for an instant win.

How do I play online with friends?

From the main menu choose "Multiplayer", create a room and share the room code, or enter a friend's room code to join.

Is the game free?

Yes, the game is free to play and supported by ads. It is for entertainment only and does not involve any real-money gambling.

How do I change the language?

Main menu > Settings > Language. Choose Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or English.

What if I get disconnected?

Check your internet connection and re-join the room. If the issue persists, email us with the approximate time it happened.
